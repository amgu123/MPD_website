Why translation matters

Why translation matters
in media monitoring

India's news landscape is multilingual, with important stories breaking in regional languages every day. Translation helps your team understand the complete picture, not just what appears in English.

  • Discover complete coverage

    Key developments often appear first in regional media. Translation helps you catch the full story.

  • Enable multi-language teams

    Everyone in your organisation can read and use the same insights, regardless of language preferences.

  • Simplify review and analysis

    Translated news makes it easier to compare coverage, identify trends and draw faster, clearer insights.

  • Share insights widely

    Translated content can be easily shared across internal teams and with clients, improving alignment and decision-making.

Use Cases

Used across monitoring workflows

News translation fits seamlessly into your daily monitoring and communication processes.

  • Management review

    Get translated summaries of key regional coverage for leadership updates and strategic discussions.

  • PR sharing

    Share translated clippings with internal stakeholders and external partners to show wider media coverage across languages.

  • Multi-city teams

    Help distributed teams across India stay aligned with local media coverage, with content available in a common language.

  • Client reporting

    Include translated regional coverage in client reports to provide a more complete and contextual view of media narratives.
